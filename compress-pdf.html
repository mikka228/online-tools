What is Compress PDF?

Compress PDF reduces file size so documents are easier to email, upload to forms, or store. This version optimizes the PDF structure (object streams) in your browser. It does not re-encode every image at a lower quality — that kind of compression needs heavier processing and is limited in pure client-side tools.

How to compress a PDF

  • Select or drop one PDF
  • Click Compress PDF
  • Compare the original and new size, then download

What results to expect

  • Best case: files with redundant objects or older structure shrink noticeably
  • Typical case: moderate reduction or a small change
  • Already optimized scans/photos: size may barely change — the file was already efficient

If you need aggressive photo compression inside a PDF, a desktop app with image re-encoding is usually stronger. This tool focuses on privacy and convenience for everyday size cleanup.

Privacy

Compression runs locally. Your PDF is not uploaded to our servers or any third party.

FAQ

Why is my file almost the same size?
The PDF may already be optimized, or most of its weight is in images that this mode does not re-encode.

Is text quality affected?
No. Text and vector content are preserved.

Is there a size limit?
Only what your device can load into memory.
