What is Rotate PDF?

Rotate one or more pages of a PDF by 90°, 180° or 270° without uploading the file. Useful when a scanned document is sideways or upside down.

How to use

  • Select or drop a PDF
  • Choose the rotation angle
  • Optionally specify pages (e.g. 1,3,5-8)
  • Download the rotated PDF

Privacy

Rotation is done with pdf-lib in your browser. The file is not sent to any server.
