Create QR codes for links, text and Wi-Fi. Download as PNG.

What is a QR code generator?

Create a QR code from any text or URL and download it as a PNG image. Everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  • Enter a link or text
  • Choose size, colors and error correction
  • Download the PNG

Privacy

Generation happens entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

FAQ

Can I make a Wi-Fi QR?
Yes. Use the format: WIFI:T:WPA;S:NetworkName;P:Password;;

Why error correction?
Higher levels survive more damage or logo overlays, but make denser codes.

Is this private?
Yes. The library runs locally from our site, not from a third-party CDN.
