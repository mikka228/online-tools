Create a QR code in seconds

What is a QR Code Generator?

Create QR codes for links, plain text, or Wi-Fi credentials and download them as PNG images. Useful for sharing URLs, contact info, or network access without typing.

How to use

  • Enter text, a URL, or Wi-Fi details
  • Optionally change colors and size
  • Download the PNG

Privacy

Generation happens in your browser. Your text is not sent to any server.
