URL Encoder / Decoder

Percent-encode and decode URL strings

What is URL encoding?

URL encoding (percent-encoding) replaces unsafe characters with %XX sequences so they can be used safely in links and query strings.

How to use

  • Paste text or a full URL
  • Press Encode or Decode
  • Copy the result

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

FAQ

When do I need URL encoding?
When putting spaces or special characters into query strings.

Encode vs decode?
Encode turns text into percent-encoded form. Decode reverses it.

Is my URL sent to a server?
No. Encoding runs in the browser.
