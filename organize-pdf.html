What is Organize PDF?

Organize PDF helps you reorder pages, rotate them, and delete the ones you do not need — then download a clean new file.

How to use

  • Open a PDF to see page thumbnails
  • Use ↑ ↓ buttons (or drag on desktop) to change order
  • Rotate pages left/right or delete unwanted ones
  • Click Download PDF

Features

  • Visual page thumbnails
  • Reorder, rotate 90°, and delete pages
  • Works on phone and desktop
  • Private — nothing is uploaded

Privacy

Your document stays on your device. Preview and editing use pdf.js and pdf-lib in the browser only.

FAQ

Can I rotate only some pages?
Yes. Select pages (or use the buttons on each thumbnail) and rotate only those.

Does reordering change the original file?
No. You download a new PDF; the original on your device is untouched.
