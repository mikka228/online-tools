Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Summary

Online Tools processes your files entirely in your browser. We do not upload your documents, images or text to our servers for processing.

What we do not collect

  • We do not receive the content of the files you open in the tools
  • We do not create user accounts
  • We do not store your PDFs, images or text on our side

Technical data

Like most websites, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and any analytics or CDN services we may use can see standard request data (IP address, user agent, pages visited). This is independent of the file processing that happens locally in your browser.

Local storage

Some tools may use the browser’s localStorage or sessionStorage to remember recent settings or history. This data stays on your device and can be cleared by you at any time through browser settings.

Third-party scripts

Some pages load open-source libraries from public CDNs (for example pdf-lib, QR libraries). These libraries run in your browser. We choose well-known libraries and prefer minimal dependencies.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy, use the Contact page or open an issue on GitHub.
