What is Batch Image Compressor?

Select dozens of photos, set quality and optional max width, then compress them all in one go. Download the results as a single ZIP archive. Everything runs in your browser.

How to use

  • Drop or select multiple images
  • Choose quality and format
  • Optionally limit max width
  • Press Compress All and wait for the progress
  • Download the ZIP

Privacy

No files are uploaded. Compression and ZIP creation happen entirely on your device.
