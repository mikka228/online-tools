What is Image Converter?

Convert an image from one format to another (JPG ↔ PNG ↔ WebP) entirely in your browser. Useful when a site only accepts a specific format or when you want smaller WebP files.

How to use

  • Select or drop an image
  • Choose the target format
  • Adjust quality for JPEG/WebP if needed
  • Convert and download

Format tips

  • JPEG — good for photos, smaller size
  • PNG — lossless, keeps transparency
  • WebP — often the smallest at similar quality

Privacy

Conversion uses the Canvas API on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
