What is Merge PDF?

Merge PDF combines multiple PDF files into a single document. You choose the order, click a button, and download the result. Everything runs in your browser — files are never uploaded to a server.

How to use

  • Drop or select two or more PDF files
  • Arrange them in the order you need (top to bottom)
  • Click Merge PDFs and download the combined file

Features

  • Unlimited number of files (limited only by your device memory)
  • Preserves original page quality and order
  • No registration, watermarks, or daily limits
  • Works offline after the page is loaded

Privacy

Your PDFs stay on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers or any third party. Processing uses the open-source pdf-lib library in your browser.

FAQ

Is there a file size limit?
There is no artificial limit. Very large files may fail on phones with little free RAM.

Does it work with encrypted PDFs?
Password-protected or encrypted PDFs may not open. Remove protection first if possible.

Can I merge PDFs on my phone?
Yes. The tool is mobile-friendly, but large merges work better on desktop.
