What is Split PDF?

Split PDF extracts pages from a document or saves each page as its own file. Common uses: sending only the signed pages of a contract, splitting a scanned pack into single pages, or pulling a chapter out of a longer PDF.

How to split or extract pages

  • Open a PDF in the tool
  • To extract a range, enter pages like 1-3, 5, 8-10 and confirm
  • To get one file per page, use Split into individual pages

Only clean numbers and ranges are accepted (for example 2 or 4-7). Values like 2x or 3.5 are ignored so you do not get surprising results.

Mobile tip

Some phone browsers block many downloads at once. If you split a long PDF into individual pages, allow multiple downloads when the browser asks — or extract a smaller range instead.

Privacy

Splitting is done in your browser with pdf-lib. Files are not uploaded.

FAQ

Can I extract non-continuous pages?
Yes. Example: 1, 3, 9-11.

Does splitting change the original?
No. You download new files; the original stays as is.

Encrypted PDFs?
They may not load until the password is removed.
