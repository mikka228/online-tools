What is Unix Timestamp Converter?

Convert Unix timestamps (seconds since 1970-01-01 UTC) to human-readable dates and the other way around. Shows the same moment in several common time zones.

How to use

  • Enter a timestamp and convert to date (Local + UTC + popular zones)
  • Or pick a date and time and get the timestamp
  • Use “Now” for the current second

Privacy

All conversion is done on your device.
